\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We present LAVi, a low-altitude visual world model combining camera-annotated data construction with joint RGB--depth generation.
Simulator-recorded trajectories guide selection and scale calibration of the annotation pipeline, while depth supervision provides explicit geometric targets during camera-conditioned training.
Direct causal adaptation enables few-step generation of paired RGB and depth observations along prescribed trajectories.
Experiments demonstrate improved visual fidelity and relative camera-control accuracy over the evaluated TI2V baselines, supporting low-altitude simulation with controllable viewpoints and explicit geometry.
